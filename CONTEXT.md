# CONTEXT.md

Glossary for this repo. Devoid of implementation detail — terms only.

## Terms

- **Portfolio** — The single-page website that sells Naman's website-building ability. The primary artifact; everything else exists to support it.
- **Showcase** — A deliberately designed example website presented in the portfolio as work Naman made. Each showcase proves a distinct capability and deploys as an independent site. Three exist in v1: an Indian-business conversion landing page, a global-feeling brand/scroll-storytelling page, and a polished dashboard.
- **The proof principle** — The portfolio must not merely claim taste and capability; it must demonstrate them. Every claim on the page (hierarchy, motion, color, maintainability) is evidenced by the page itself.
- **The send test** — The bar for done: "Would I confidently send this website to someone who could pay me ₹20,000–₹25,000 for a website?" Sendable without explaining anything away.
- **Sendable v1** — The first package that passes the send test: portfolio + all three showcases, live, no placeholders, no "coming soon".
- **Art direction** — A concrete visual identity (typography, color, composition, motion, density, mood) expressed as a buildable sample, not a written description. Chosen by reacting to artifacts.
- **Attention management** — The design discipline that not everything pops; motion, color, scale, contrast, whitespace serve hierarchy and intentional eye movement.
