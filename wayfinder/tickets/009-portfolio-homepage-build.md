## Question

(Can span sessions) Build the single-page portfolio in the chosen art direction with the drafted copy: full scroll experience, typography, color system, motion, composition. This page is the primary sales artifact and must itself demonstrate every capability it claims — the proof principle. Every section is built against the **critique checklist** (purpose, hierarchy, typography, composition, image, color, motion, whitespace, interaction, responsive, accessibility, originality), with attention allocation decided per section and every major animation answering "what does this movement communicate?" Every major section gets a deliberate mobile composition — "it technically fits" is failure. Accessibility constraints (WCAG 2.2: contrast, reflow, text spacing, keyboard, focus, target size, reduced motion) are designed in, not patched later. Showcase sections are built as slots (placeholders that are structurally complete, not visibly unfinished); their content is filled by a later ticket once the showcases exist.
