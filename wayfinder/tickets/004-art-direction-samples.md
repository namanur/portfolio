# Three art directions — pick the portfolio's visual identity

## Question

Which visual direction does the portfolio take? Produce 2–3 rough but concrete art-direction samples that differ meaningfully in typography, color, composition, motion, density, and mood — genuinely different creative directions, not three generic templates. Each direction must include at minimum: a hero, its typography system, a primary color system, one project/showcase presentation, one transition/scroll interaction, and one image treatment. **Color constraint (decided)**: all directions build on a monochromatic neutral base with green and orange accents — the directions differentiate on typography, composition, motion, and density, and each proposes its own specific green/orange values and accent roles. Each is evaluated **using the Design Research Brief and critique checklist** — hierarchy, attention management, hero framework, anti-vibecode watchlist — not on "this looks cool." Naman reacts to the artifacts and picks the strongest; the chosen direction is then developed deeply and governs every visual decision downstream.
